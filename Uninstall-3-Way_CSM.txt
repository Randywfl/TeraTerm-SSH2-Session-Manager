3-Way_CSM Has no uninstaller.
Follow these steps to complete a manual uninstall.

If you wish to remove 3-Way_CSM From your computer.
1. Open your 3-Way_CSM app and click the [Sessions Folder] button.
 a. Delete every file that begins with the name sessions.
 b. Delete every file that ends with .ttl 

2. Open Explorer and browse to where your 3-Way_CSM folder resides. Highlight that folder, Hold the shift key and press delete. Confirm you want to permanently remove 3-Way_CSM from your computer.  There were no registry entries on your computer so you have now removed every trace of 3-Way_CSM from your computer.


3. TeraTerm was installed to your Program Files directory. If you do not want it anymore, It does have its own uninstallers, so you should uninstall TeraTerm In the traditional fashion. I recommend. Using "Revo uninstaller" to get the best cleanup possible.
